Meet Our Distinguished Head Dentist

Dr. Samuel Siyoum — Head Dentist

Dr. Samuel Siyoum leads Peacock Dental Ethiopia with a commitment to ethical, patient-centered, and modern dental care. His approach combines clinical excellence, precision, and compassion, ensuring that every patient receives personalized treatment in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Dedicated to continuous learning and the adoption of advanced dental technologies, he helps patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry.

Credentials: Dr. Samuel Siyoum brings over 15 years of clinical experience to Peacock Dental Ethiopia. He earned his dental degree from the prestigious Addis Ababa University, one of Ethiopia's leading institutions, and has since built a reputation for advanced, patient-focused care.
