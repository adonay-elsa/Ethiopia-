Comprehensive Dental Care

Dental services built for comfort, confidence, and lasting results.

Peacock Dental offers a complete range of modern dental care in Asosa, Ethiopia. Our services combine compassionate treatment, advanced technology, and skilled professionals so every visit becomes a smoother path to a healthier smile.

Our Services

Comprehensive dental care delivered with compassion and modern techniques — from preventive checkups to specialized restorative and cosmetic treatments. Our service range is informed by the leading standards of Dr. Emibet Dental Clinic, Ethiopia's pioneer in advanced dental care.

Preventive Care

Routine exams, professional cleaning, fluoride protection, and patient education to keep your smile healthy and avoid future treatment. Following the proactive screening model used at Dr. Emibet Dental Clinic.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Whitening, smile-enhancing restorations, and aesthetic consultation to help you achieve a brighter, confident smile. Includes bleaching and veneer options modeled on leading Ethiopian cosmetic practices.

Restorative Care

Fillings, crowns, bridges, and full-mouth rehabilitation designed to restore strength, function, and appearance. We use durable composite, porcelain, and zirconia materials for lasting results.

Oral Surgery & Tooth Extraction

Safe extraction, wisdom tooth removal, and surgical care with modern techniques and gentle post-operative support. From normal extractions to surgical procedures, your comfort is our priority.

Orthodontic Treatment

Traditional braces and modern aligner solutions for patients of all ages. Improve alignment, bite, and smile confidence with personalized orthodontic planning.

Pediatric & Family Dentistry

Family-friendly dental care for children and adults, with a warm clinic atmosphere and easy appointment support. Gentle approaches tailored for young patients.

Root Canal Treatment

Expert endodontic care to save natural teeth from severe decay or infection. Using modern techniques for a comfortable, efficient procedure that preserves your smile.

Dental Implants

Permanent tooth replacement options that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Includes bone graft support for ideal implant stability and long-term success.

Periodontal Care

Deep scaling, root planing, and gum disease treatment to protect the foundation of your smile. Preventive periodontal therapy for healthy gums and lasting oral health.
