Why Peacock Dental Ethiopia Matters

We bring gentle, modern dental care to Asosa with attention, trust, and patient comfort at the center of every visit. Our small team combines local knowledge with advanced treatment options so you can feel valued, informed, and confident about your smile.

From preventive cleanings to restorative care, we focus on strong long-term results and a caring experience that makes every patient feel understood.

Services We Provide

General Dentistry

Routine exams, cleanings, and preventive care to keep your smile healthy.

Restorative Treatment

Fillings, crowns, and tooth repair designed for strength, comfort, and natural appearance.

Cosmetic Care

Tooth whitening and smile enhancement that help you feel proud of your smile.

Emergency Support

Responsive care for urgent pain, trauma, and dental problems when you need help quickly.
